iQiQGame Labs

Standalone puzzle projects, each with its own rules page and a playable build.

Projects

  • Sudoku Puzzle — classic 9×9 Sudoku plus the Kropki variant, where dot markers between cells tell you whether the neighbouring digits are consecutive or in a 1:2 ratio. Play the full version as a Sudoku game in your browser, with a new grid published every day.
  • 24 Points Game — four numbers on the board, combine them with + − × ÷ so every hand totals exactly 24. Short rounds, no timer, good for warming up mental arithmetic. Try the 24 points game online when you only have a couple of minutes.

About

Every project here is built to run in a normal browser tab — no install, no sign-up, no account. The games are also hosted on iQiQGame, alongside a larger library of logic puzzles, typing and click-speed trainers, and daily number challenges.
